01 / About me

Curious by nature.
Persistent in practice.

I enjoy learning how things work, solving problems as they come up, and improving a project step by step.

I’m a System Administrator and Generative AI Content Creator with a B.S. in Computer Science from Adamson University. My background includes Linux system administration, troubleshooting, and wireless technologies.

I combine prompt engineering, AI tools, and traditional video editing to create music, visuals, cinematic stories, and promotional social media content. My work brings together technical problem-solving, creative direction, and an understanding of direct response marketing.

Story & visual development

Shattered Heavens

Ongoing project

An ongoing story universe, expanding into a game prototype.

Shattered Heavens is my ongoing creative universe, shared through the Geno AiniSaga channel. It combines anime-inspired artwork, character designs, world history, and storyboards. The work now also includes an early fighting-game prototype based on the same creative world.

The project connects visual storytelling with interactive development. The supplied sample presents Episode 12 alongside prototype game footage, including character combat, health bars, and special-move effects. Both the story and game remain in development.

  • Character design
  • Story development
  • AI-assisted art
  • Game prototype
What this project involves
  • Developing the look and identity of characters.
  • Building the background, relationships, and history of the story world.
  • Planning scenes through storyboards and sharing the ongoing development.
  • Exploring how characters and visual ideas can carry into a fighting-game prototype.

Explore Shattered Heavens on YouTube

AI-assisted filmmaking

Schwartz AI Films

Published creative work

Short cinematic stories made with creative direction and AI tools.

Schwartz AI Films is my creative filmmaking project for cinematic stories and character-led short films. Its subjects include martial arts, superheroes, and adventure, using AI-assisted visuals to explore original characters and imagined worlds.

Music & conversation

AMUSZYNC

Published channel

Conversations about music and the memories it brings back.

AMUSZYNC is my music-focused content project. It features Tito Aries talking about songs across different genres, generations, and places, with attention to the feelings and personal memories connected to them.

Rather than focusing only on musical terms, the channel makes music a starting point for familiar stories and conversation. Its subjects invite listeners to reflect on songs that bring joy, comfort, nostalgia, or energy.

  • Music commentary
  • Stories & memories
  • YouTube content
What this project involves

Product & promotional video

AI UGC Videos

AI-generated sample work

Product videos presented in a familiar, conversational style.

These samples explore AI-created videos in the style of user-generated content (UGC): product presentations that feel like a creator speaking directly to the viewer. The format demonstrates how a product can be introduced through a clear visual sequence, with attention to the presenter, the product, and the setting. It is a creative demonstration of AI-assisted promotional content.

What this project involves

  • Presenting the sample in a portrait format suited to mobile viewing.

Application development

SchwarZtream

Features being updated

A personal media library you can browse and watch on TV or mobile.

SchwarZtream is my personal media application project, previously called SchwartzFlix. It brings movies, series, concerts, and music videos into a browsable library, with a server supplying the media to TV and mobile players.

The goal is to make a personal collection easier to organise and enjoy. Development includes navigation with a TV remote, subtitles, and continuing a video from where it was left off, with ongoing improvements to playback and usability.

  • Android TV
  • Mobile player
  • Media server
What this project involves
  • Developing the connection between the server, TV player, and mobile interface.
  • Organising media into categories and browsing views.
  • Improving remote-control navigation, subtitle handling, and playback behaviour.
